# Scraper v2: decisions

One rule per entry, with the reason. Each is easy to reverse. The spec's own "Decisions already made" (SPEC.md) are not repeated here.

1. **Refactor, not rewrite.** One of the spec's five rewrite criteria is true (the cron-ats router mixes orchestration, normalization and writes); the table is in PLAN.md. The write path moves into `backend/ingest/core/pipeline.py`; `ATSJob`, `BoardSnapshot` and reconciliation stay.
2. **Adapters return `(list[ATSJob], complete, total)`, not an async iterator of RawJob.** Reconciliation needs the whole board and a completeness flag; `ATSJob` already is the raw job every downstream function takes.
3. **Career families are stored as slugs** (`healthcare_clinical`), with labels in the UI. Five of the twenty labels contain commas, and every list filter on `GET /jobs` is comma-separated.
4. **The old "New Grad / Entry Level" choice maps to Intern/New Grad plus Entry Level.** That is what the option meant. The student-feed gate (spec: at least 5,000) therefore counts Intern/New Grad plus Entry Level: the weak entry tier (junior, level I, associate) moves from `new_grad` to `entry`, matching Jobright's split.
5. **A posting missing from a complete board listing is still removed at once** (hidden and closed with `closed_reason` `delisted`), and revived if it is listed again. The spec's two-step stale-then-closed exists to survive a flaky listing; today's revive-on-reappearance already does that, and it is tested.
6. **Classification runs inline at insert, not as queued enrich tasks.** Rules cost microseconds per job; a queue stage would add writes and transfer for nothing. `enrichment_version` still records which rules tagged a row.
7. **`stale` rows are hidden from the feed and from alerts** (spec). There were 0 stale rows on 2026-10-25, so nothing disappears on deploy.
8. **Full re-list once a day.** With every level, a board's stored rows grow about tenfold, and the refresh reads them back: re-crawling hourly would cost gigabytes of Neon transfer a month.
9. **The fingerprint is a match key, not a unique index.** One employer posts identical titles as separate requisitions; uniqueness stays on `url` and `external_id`.
10. **A new registry field, `hq_country`, says where an employer is based; `country` keeps its old meaning.** `country` on a registry entry tells the crawler that every posting on the board is in that country, and it skips the location filter for that board. Put on a multinational (RBC, Pfizer), it would file their London and Zurich jobs under Canada or the US. So every entry gets `hq_country` (the spec's "at least 250 Canadian and 350 US employers" counts it), and `country` is set only on single-country boards: hospitals, universities, school boards, governments and regional utilities.
11. **Storage caps sized for the free plan: 60 live rows per employer, inserts stop at 400 MB.** About 1,100 employers share 512 MB; Neon's logical size runs about 36 MB above `pg_database_size`, so 400 keeps a branch near 436 MB, under the 450 MB gate. Raise both after upgrading the plan.
12. **A balanced subset of the research boards is enabled.** All Canadian boards and all US institutions; US private boards per sector up to a cap, biggest first. 431 verified US boards are held with "held: storage budget".
13. **The tag backfill never lifts a stored internship or new-grad row above Entry Level.** A title-only recompute moved 361 such rows to mid or above in a dry run, but they had passed the old filter on department or commitment evidence the backfill cannot see. Their board's next crawl relabels them with full information.
14. **Oracle Cloud Recruiting, iCIMS and Jibe boards were held, then re-enabled after their adapters' `complete` flags were fixed.** The Task 4 review found they could call a listing complete when the source omitted its total, and a complete listing removes every stored row it did not see. Fix round 1 made `complete` need positive evidence in all five Tier 2 adapters, and the 221 boards (108 Oracle, 46 iCIMS, 67 Jibe) are enabled now: 1,315 enabled in all. Taleo and SuccessFactors still have no enabled boards.
15. **Tasks 1, 6 and 11 skipped their per-task review; the final whole-branch review covers them.** Two usage-limit stops cut the night into three windows.
16. **The board research stopped at four segments.** Every target was exceeded (1,533 verified boards) before the first usage stop.
17. **The scheduled runner is opt-in: `ingest.yml` does nothing until the `INGEST_DATABASE_URL` secret exists.** Until then it ends green with a notice, so an unconfigured repository never shows a red run, and adding the secret is the moment its spending starts. It runs once a day (06:23 UTC), not every 6 hours as the spec's example had it, for the transfer sums of decision 8.
18. **The workflow runs the runner with `--no-schema`.** Its Neon role can SELECT, INSERT and UPDATE the ingestion tables and run no DDL, because the secret sits in a public repository. The runner's default schema step sends `CREATE INDEX IF NOT EXISTS` for tables that role does not own, and Postgres refuses that even when the index exists ("must be owner of table cover_letters"). So the app applies the schema when it deploys, and a schema that is behind the code fails the run red, never silently: deploy the app before the first run.
19. **The cut-over window is 20 hours.** With `INGEST_V2_RUNNER` on, `cron-ats` leaves a board stamped in the last 20 hours to the daily runner, and a board the runner missed is picked up once 20 hours have passed, so a dropped fire is covered within the day. The price is about four hours before each fire in which `cron-ats` may crawl a board the runner is about to crawl again; the repeat is idempotent and bounded to one shard per run. A 27-hour window would remove the overlap and slow the fallback.
20. **The legacy `entry` filter key now means Entry Level only.** Under the old taxonomy it meant internship plus new grad. The six options are Jobright's and `entry` has to name the tier between new grad and mid, so a bookmarked `?experience_level=entry`, or an `entry` saved before scraper v2, shows the entry tier. That is deliberate. The old "New Grad / Entry Level" choice keeps its meaning through the legacy `new_grad` key (decision 4), and internship plus new grad are the `intern_new_grad` option.
21. **`CRON_ATS_V2_CATALOGUE` (default off) holds the every-level catalogue back.** Without it, merging turned on every-level ingestion in production with nothing able to stop it: `cron-ats` would crawl all 1,309 crawlable boards at every level, reading back about 1.7 GB a month for the refresh on top of the 4.1 of 5 GB the project already uses, and the new rows would reach the 400 MB insert stop within weeks, after which no board, internships included, would insert anything. Off, `cron-ats` keeps the student catalogue on the five older platforms through the shared pipeline, as before scraper v2. On, it stores every level and crawls the adapters' boards. Turn it on after the Neon plan upgrade and the raised `limits.json` caps (decision 11), and after `INGEST_V2_RUNNER`.
22. **The Jobs page and onboarding offer only the experience levels that have live jobs, and the stats and the right rail count the viewer's levels.** While decision 21's switch is off, production stores only internship and new grad rows, and a Senior or Director option would be a silent dead end that returns an empty feed. `GET /jobs/levels` lists the options with live rows (cached for five minutes), and the frontend builds its list from it in the same order with the same labels. A saved level whose option is hidden is dropped from the active filter, and an unreadable answer offers all six. `GET /jobs/stats` and `GET /jobs/sidebar` take the viewer's levels (Intern/New Grad plus Entry Level when none are given), so a total or the "hiring the most" list is never driven by roles the feed hides.
